Does a knowledge graph actually help? starting fall 2026

Every "AI company brain" assumes a knowledge graph improves answers over decision records. Nobody seems to have measured it. I'm benchmarking plain hybrid retrieval against GraphRAG on the Rust RFC corpus, with hand-written questions. If the graph doesn't help, that's the result and I'll report it.
