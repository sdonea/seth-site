  1. PageMD

    Founder · the pager on the nightstand

    An AI agent that triages inbound calls and pages on-call staff at outpatient clinics. My mom is a doctor and her pager kept interrupting dinner. Doctors can't be taken out of the loop, so we build for the nurses and staff on the other end.

  2. Holomap

    Builder · the glowing chart above the bed

    A straight line is the shortest way across the sea, but rarely the cheapest. Holomap pulls today's ocean currents, the 16-day wind forecast and live ship positions, draws them on a glowing tactical table, and plans the route that spends the least fuel.

    Every day a GitHub Action runs the planner on 58 trips off the US East Coast and posts the biggest saving. holomap.app

  3. Dove

    Builder · the drone flying around the room

    A drone that works as a police officer's sidekick: it keeps eyes on a subject so nobody goes in blind, and it decides nothing about people. Tell it "follow the person in the red hoodie" and it finds them with its own camera, follows from 20–30 m, searches when they duck behind a building, picks them back up, and flies home on command. No face recognition; it only knows people by what they're wearing.

    Simulation only so far: a PX4 autopilot in Gazebo, YOLO tracking, and a safety supervisor that every command passes through. In the scored demo it found the target again 7 seconds after losing them, with zero safety violations. dove-kohl.vercel.app

  4. Field Dex

    Builder · the cards on the bed

    Enter a US ZIP code and every species people logged on iNaturalist within 10 km this week becomes a collectible card, rarest first. The dove on your feeder is Common; the fly someone photographed down the road might be one of a few hundred ever logged.

    Every evening a GitHub Action posts the rarest find near Evansville. fielddex-delta.vercel.app
